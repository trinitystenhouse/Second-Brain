## The Self-Interview

Fourteen parts. This is the thing that turns a folder of notes into a second brain that actually knows how you think.

**How to do it.** Open Claude in this vault and say `onboard me` — it runs the interview as a conversation, a few questions at a time, and writes your answers into the `_self/` files for you. Or ignore Claude entirely and write straight into the files yourself; the mapping is shown in brackets under each part.

**Rules of engagement.** Go at your pace. Skip anything. Come back to anything. Stop halfway through a part if you want to — progress is tracked, and nothing is lost. Answer in whatever register is natural to you; Claude keeps your phrasings, because your voice is part of what it is learning.

**Honest beats polished.** Nobody is marking this. A vague, flattering answer produces a vague, flattering second brain. The parts you least want to write down are usually the ones that make the biggest difference to the quality of advice you get back.

**Where it goes.** Into `_self/` on your disk. `_self/` is git-ignored, so it is never committed or pushed anywhere. Read [PRIVACY_AND_SECURITY.md](../PRIVACY_AND_SECURITY.md) if you want the full picture before you start.

**If you only do three parts:** 1, 7 and 14. Identity, values and how you want this thing to treat you.

---

### Part 1 — Identity & positioning → `identity.md`

1.1 Who are you, in one honest paragraph? Not the LinkedIn version.
1.2 Who are you trying to become over the next few years?
1.3 What do people come to you for?
1.4 What makes your perspective unusual — the thing you see that others in your field or situation tend not to?
1.5 What would you want to be known for?
1.6 What do people consistently get wrong about you?

### Part 2 — Roots & background → `roots-and-background.md`

2.1 Where are you from, and how much does that still shape how you operate?
2.2 What did the people who raised you believe about work, money and success? What did you keep, and what did you deliberately put down?
2.3 What was expected of you growing up — said out loud, and unsaid?
2.4 Are there worlds you move between that pull you in different directions? What does each one ask of you?
2.5 What formative experience explains more about you than your CV does?

### Part 3 — Projects & work → `projects.md`

3.1 Inventory everything you are working on right now — paid, unpaid, serious, silly.
3.2 And everything you have worked on before that still matters to how you think.
3.3 What patterns repeat? What do you reliably start, finish, or abandon — and at what point does the abandoning usually happen?
3.4 What have you paused that still tugs at you?
3.5 The dream project, if failure were impossible and nobody was watching?

### Part 4 — Skills & capabilities → `skills-inventory.md`

4.1 What can you actually do today, at a level you would be comfortable being tested on? Be precise rather than modest or inflated.
4.2 What can you do passably that you would like to do well?
4.3 What do you want to be able to do in a year?
4.4 What have you decided *not* to learn, and why? This is as informative as the list above it.
4.5 Your current tools and stack — everything you actually use, not what you think you should use.

### Part 5 — Craft & creative practice → `craft.md`

*If you make things — music, writing, code, images, food, events, anything. Skip if nothing here applies.*

5.1 What do you make, and why does it matter to you? The real reason, not the presentable one.
5.2 Describe your taste. What do you make when nobody is asking for anything?
5.3 Whose work do you measure yourself against, and what specifically about it?
5.4 Where are you on the curve right now, honestly — and what is the actual bottleneck between here and better?
5.5 Ambition check: hobby forever, serious side-practice, or a lane you want to make a living in?

### Part 6 — Money & work economics → `money-mindset.md`

6.1 What is your honest relationship with money — earning it, spending it, worrying about it, talking about it?
6.2 What did you absorb about money before you had any say in the matter?
6.3 What does financial security actually mean to you, in numbers or in circumstances?
6.4 What would you do differently if money were not a constraint? And which of those things could you do anyway?
6.5 What are you currently trading for money that you would rather not be?

### Part 7 — Decisions & values → `values-decisions.md`

7.1 How do you actually make big decisions? Walk through a recent one, including the messy part.
7.2 What do you consistently say no to? Consistently yes to?
7.3 Your core values — the genuine non-negotiables, not the ones that sound good.
7.4 Where do your values contradict each other? Everyone's do, and knowing where is useful.
7.5 What is a decision you got wrong, and what rule did you make from it?
7.6 Whose judgement do you trust more than your own, and on what?

### Part 8 — Relationships & people close to you → `relationships.md`

*One of the two heavier parts. Valuable, and entirely optional.*

8.1 Who are the most important people in your life right now, and what does each relationship need from you?
8.2 What have past relationships — of any kind — taught you about yourself?
8.3 What sets off the alarm in your head, and what does the alarm make you do?
8.4 After a conflict, how do you repair? How would you *like* to repair?
8.5 What kind of person do you want to be to the people close to you, and where is the gap right now?

### Part 9 — Mind & body → `mind-body.md`

*The other heavy one. Also optional. This file stays on your disk, and Claude is instructed never to diagnose or moralise about anything in it.*

9.1 Energy audit: sleep, movement, food, screens, alcohol, work hours — what is actually true right now, rather than what you intend?
9.2 When are you at your sharpest in a day? When are you useless?
9.3 How do you know when you are not okay? What are your tells, and who notices before you do?
9.4 What do you do to recover, and does it actually work?
9.5 What would be different in a year if you looked after yourself properly?

### Part 10 — Network & communities → `network.md`

10.1 Who is genuinely in your corner — who would you call at 3am?
10.2 Mentors and people you learn from: who, and what do you take from each?
10.3 Which scenes, fields or communities are you part of? Which do you want into, and what is the barrier?
10.4 Who should you be talking to that you currently are not? What is stopping you?
10.5 What do you offer other people, concretely? What do you need from them?

### Part 11 — Inputs & consumption → `consumption.md`

11.1 Your information diet — what do you actually read, watch and listen to? Be honest, including the junk.
11.2 What inputs have genuinely changed how you think?
11.3 Where are the gaps you keep meaning to fill?
11.4 What would you like to consume less of?

### Part 12 — How you work → `operations.md`

12.1 When and where do you do your best work? Under what conditions does it happen reliably?
12.2 What are your bottlenecks — the things that stall you, every time?
12.3 What systems or routines have ever actually stuck, and what did they have in common?
12.4 What does procrastination look like for you specifically? What is it usually protecting you from?
12.5 How do you currently keep track of things, and where does that break down?

### Part 13 — Aspirations & constraints → `aspirations.md`

13.1 Describe an ideal ordinary week, three years from now. The ordinary part matters more than the highlights.
13.2 What would make the next twelve months a success, concretely enough to check?
13.3 Your real fears about all of this — not the presentable ones.
13.4 Hard constraints you are not willing to violate: time, place, people, ethics, health.
13.5 What are you willing to be bad at in order to be good at the things above?

### Part 14 — Meta: this system → `system-rules.md`

14.1 What do you want this second brain to do for you? Be specific — "help me think" is not actionable.
14.2 How do you want Claude to talk to you? Blunt or gentle, brief or thorough, challenging or supportive?
14.3 What should it never do?
14.4 Which topics are off-limits, or need handling with particular care?
14.5 How will you know, in six months, that this has been worth the effort?

## After the interview

`kg-brain` unlocks: from then on, when you ask for advice, a draft, or a decision, Claude reads your `_self/` layer first and filters its answer through what you actually said, quoting the lines it is applying.

Try it immediately with something real. A good first test is a decision you are genuinely stuck on — you will see straight away whether the answers you gave were honest enough to be useful.

Your answers are not fixed. Revise any file any time, or say `update my interview` to redo a part. The vault is supposed to change as you do.
