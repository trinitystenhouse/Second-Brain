## What lives here

`_self/` is the layer that makes this vault *yours*. Everything else — the spokes, the research, the news filtering — reads from here to decide what matters and how to talk to you.

It starts empty. It fills up when you do the self-interview: open Claude in this vault and say `onboard me`, or write into the files yourself using [self-interview.md](self-interview.md) as the prompt list.

## The files

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `identity.md` | Who you are and who you are becoming |
| `roots-and-background.md` | Where you come from and what it left you with |
| `projects.md` | Everything you are working on, and your patterns around finishing |
| `skills-inventory.md` | What you can do, want to do, and have decided not to learn |
| `craft.md` | Your creative practice, taste and ambition for it |
| `money-mindset.md` | Your relationship with money and what security means to you |
| `values-decisions.md` | Non-negotiables and how you actually decide |
| `relationships.md` | The people who matter and how you handle conflict |
| `mind-body.md` | Energy, tells, recovery |
| `network.md` | Who is in your corner, who you want to reach |
| `consumption.md` | Your information diet |
| `operations.md` | When and how you work well, and what stalls you |
| `aspirations.md` | Where this is all going, and the constraints on it |
| `system-rules.md` | How you want Claude to behave. Overrides everything else |
| `interview-progress.md` | Which parts you have done. Created on first run |

Only `self-interview.md` and this README are tracked by git. Everything else you write here is ignored, so your personal layer is never committed or pushed.

## Rules

- **Claude never modifies these files without your say-so.** It reads them constantly and writes them only through `kg-onboard` or your direct instruction.
- **Your words beat its inferences.** If something you wrote here contradicts a conclusion Claude drew elsewhere, yours wins.
- **Revise freely.** Say `update my interview` to redo a part, or just edit the file. This layer is meant to drift as you do — a `_self/` that has not changed in two years is out of date, not stable.
