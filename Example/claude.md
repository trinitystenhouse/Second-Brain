## What this spoke is for

**This is a worked example, not real content.** It exists to show the format: what a source note looks like, what a permanent note looks like, how notes link to each other, and what a map of content does. The subject is the vault method itself, so reading it teaches you the thing and demonstrates the thing at the same time.

Delete this whole folder once you have a spoke of your own. Nothing depends on it.

## Pipeline

`00_Inbox` (raw drops) → `01_Sources` (one note per real source) → `02_Literature_Notes` (atomic concepts, optional) → `03_Permanent_Notes` (your own synthesis) → `04_Maps_of_Content` (hubs with a reading order).

## Domain rules

The sources here are real and the URLs work — check them. That is the standard for every spoke: a source note corresponds to something that exists and that someone actually read. A fabricated citation is worse than a missing one, because it looks the same as a real one until the day it matters.
