## Cross-domain ideas

The most valuable ideas in a knowledge graph live *between* spokes, not inside them. Within a spoke you mostly find what you went looking for. Across two, you find the thing you had no way to go looking for — because it only exists once both halves are in the same place.

This file is the permanent record of those. The weekly ideas run (`kg-news` in weekly mode) appends its best finds here. Add your own any time; yours will usually be better, because you know which ones you would actually act on.

## What belongs here

A connection earns a line when it survives being stated plainly. The test is whether you can write it as a single claim — "the thing that makes X work is the same thing that makes Y fail" — without it dissolving into a pun or a vague resemblance. Two domains sharing a word is not a connection. Two domains sharing a mechanism is.

Each entry: the claim, the spokes it spans as `[[wikilinks]]`, and the first concrete step if there is one.
